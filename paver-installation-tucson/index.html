What we install

Paver pathways, driveways, and travertine

These are the paving services already offered by Sunset Sky. The work is outdoor flatwork in pavers and stone, including travertine. We do not install poured concrete driveways.

Paver pathways

Walkways and entries that connect the front of a property to the door, courtyard, or backyard.

Paver driveways

Driveways built with pavers or stone. This is paver installation, not a poured concrete driveway.

Travertine

Travertine for entries, patios, and courtyards. It is one of the materials listed with our landscape services.

Questions

Paver installation FAQ

What paver work does Sunset Sky install?

We install paver pathways and walkways, paver driveways, and travertine paving for homes and businesses in Tucson and the surrounding areas.

Do you install travertine?

Yes. Travertine is one of the materials listed on our site, and we install it for entries, patios, and courtyards along with our other paver work.

Do you install poured concrete driveways?

No. Our driveway work is paver and stone installation, including travertine. We do not install poured concrete driveways.

Are you licensed to do this work?

Yes. Sunset Sky Landscape is licensed, bonded, and insured. Our Arizona ROC license is CR-21 #332965. We have served Tucson and the surrounding areas since 2013.
