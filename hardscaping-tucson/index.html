Questions

Hardscaping FAQ

What hardscape do you install?

Fire pits, paver and stone patios, and the related outdoor flatwork shown in our photos. Paver pathways, driveways, and travertine are on our paver page.

Do you install fire pits?

Yes. Fire pits are a listed service. The pits we build are freestanding, and gas connections are completed by a licensed trade.

Do you build pools or load-bearing walls?

No. Swimming pools and load-bearing walls are outside our CR-21 license. Retaining walls are part of our listed hardscape work only when they stay within that license.

Are you licensed to do this work?

Yes. Sunset Sky Landscape is licensed, bonded, and insured. Our Arizona ROC license is CR-21 #332965. We have served Tucson and the surrounding areas since 2013.

How do I request a quote?

Call (520) 664-7057 or use the free quote form. We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
